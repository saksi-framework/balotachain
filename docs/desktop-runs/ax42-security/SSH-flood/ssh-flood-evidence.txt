## Reading (written by the orchestrator, 2026-10-08)
- The flood is constant background traffic from many hosting-provider IPs, each opening about one
  connection every 5 s and closing it before authentication (~340/min in total, ~2.06M from Oct 4 to Oct 7).
- It overflowed sshd's default MaxStartups 10:30:100, so our own sessions were dropped at random
  (scp resets, "kex_exchange_identification: connection abort") during the A6 upload on 2026-10-07.
- What fixed our access: MaxStartups 100:30:300 + PerSourceMaxStartups 10, and the thesis group's
  home IP exempted ahead of the per-source rate limit. After that the A6 upload completed.
- What it did NOT do: the per-source limit (10/min, burst 20) sits just above each bot's rate, so the
  flood itself continues at a similar volume (2,864 closes in the 10 min before this file). It is an
  availability nuisance, not an intrusion: password login is off (X3), so these connections cannot log in.
