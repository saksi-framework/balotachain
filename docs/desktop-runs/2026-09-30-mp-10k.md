## 5. Contention

The wizard marks a repetition **contended** when either of its two host samples (taken before generate and after verify) shows host CPU above 25 % or guest `load1` above 0.25 x 16 = 4.0. The two halves are reported separately here: **host** = a Windows host CPU sample over 25 %; **guest** = a WSL guest `load1` sample over 4.0. From 10K up the guest half is mostly the run's own generate and verify work (16 auditor threads) still in the 1-minute load average, not outside load.

Measured repetitions flagged: **10 of 10** by the wizard's rule; **host CPU: 0**, **guest load: 10** (a repetition can carry both). Highest host CPU sample on a measured repetition: 19.8 %.

Every measured repetition is flagged, **all on guest load** (self-load, load1 6.73 to 9.49); **host CPU never exceeded 25 %** on a measured repetition (maximum 19.8 %, m5 start).

## 6. Stalls

**Two stalls inside the network, with a quiet host.** m6: 460.672 TPS, p99 2,903.0 ms, a 10.6 s checkpoint gap; host 13.7 % / 1.9 %. m7: 625.552 TPS, p99 1,960.1 ms, a 9.4 s gap; host 18.2 % / 3.9 %. Every other repetition's longest gap is 1.6 to 2.4 s and its p99 272 to 423 ms. The two stalls pull the mean TPS to 762.4 (median 789.345) and the mean p99 to 775.4 ms (median 372.804). p50 is unaffected (153.2 and 153.1 ms): most ballots commit normally, and a block-commit pause of several seconds catches the tail.

**`campaign-20260929-184458-21` (attempt 1)** was flagged on 10 of 10 measured repetitions, **3 on host CPU** (m3 start 29.7 %, m9 end 26.6 %, m10 start 27.3 %) and 10 on guest load. Rerun once from reset. Its throughput fell across the campaign as one network's ledger grew (12 repetitions x 30,000 records): m1 807.8 and m2 826.5 TPS, then 776.8, 646.5, 658.8, 717.6, 756.6, 626.6, 704.7, 663.8, with p50 rising from 149 to 183 ms and no stall (longest gap 2.9 s). Median 704.677 TPS, p99 411.1 ms. **Log discrepancy:** the study log summarises reps 4 to 10 as ~630 to 720 TPS; the files give 626.6 to 756.6 (m7 is 756.6). The files are used here.

**The standing attempt does not repeat attempt 1's decline**: 761.8, 881.1, 866.6, 833.8, 873.6, then the two stalls, then 789.3, 708.3, 823.2. The within-campaign decline is therefore not reproducible at MP-10K on this evidence; the across-tier fall is (study index, finding a).

## 8. Caveats

The host samples are two points per repetition (before generate, after verify), not a trace across the submission window: a spike between them is invisible, and a flagged sample can fall outside the window it is charged to. Host CPU is the Windows host's total. The desktop was not idle: at 01:52 local the study log recorded Docker Desktop's UI (about 1.3 cores), Brave (about 1.2 cores across its processes) and an `mpv` video player running, none started by the study. `ledger_bytes_delta` is empty in every run (no producer for the column on this path). One build, one machine, one single-orderer test network. `docs/CLAIMS.md` and `docs/manuscript-amendments.md` were not edited.
