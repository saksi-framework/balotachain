## 0. Why the night stopped: the host disk filled

At 07:34 local, during SP-1M's second repetition, the host drive **Q: ran out of space** (0 GB free of 931 GB).

Docker's data disk, `docker_data.vhdx`, is a dynamically expanding VHDX that holds every Fabric volume. It lives on Q: through the junction `C:\Users\User\AppData\Local\Docker\wsl\disk` → `Q:\DockerDesktop\DockerDesktopWSL\disk`, and measured 216.8 GB afterwards. When it could not grow:
- the WSL guest logged SCSI write failures and took the disk offline (`sd 0:0:0:2: Device offlined`, `I/O error, dev sdc`, `EXT4-fs … potential data loss`);
- the peer, chaincode and containerd-shim died with SIGBUS;
- m2 lost its last 67,300 ballots, and m3 could not connect.

Docker's API has answered 500 since then.

The cause lies in the host environment, and nothing in the study checked for it:
- every ledger is kept three times (two peers and the orderer);
- a network reset does not shrink the VHDX;
- the preflight's Disk row measures the run store, and the guest's `df` of the peer volume shows the VHDX's virtual 1 TB.

The night was stopped at 08:05 under the hard rules: a Docker relaunch would come back to the same full disk. Docker was not relaunched and nothing was deleted. Details are in the [SP-1M note](2026-10-01-sp-1m.md) §3–5 and the study log.

## 2. Candidate-count check

Plan item 1. MP-1K (1,000 voters x 3 positions = 3,000 ballot records per repetition) at 4, 10 and 28 candidates per position. Everything else is the MP-1K preset: 5 trustees at threshold 3, `realistic`, on-chain, 128 in flight, closed loop. The 4-candidate row is the standing MP-1K campaign of 2026-09-30, `campaign-20260929-180218-15` (2 + 10, branch `docs/ch4-study-2026-09-30`, `docs/desktop-runs/2026-09-30-mp-1k/summary.csv`). The 10- and 28-candidate rows are tonight's standing campaigns (2 + 5).

Each figure is the `summary.csv` median over measured repetitions divided by 3,000 records:
- **proof-gen** is `proof_gen_cpu_ms`: the voter-side CDS and selection-proof generation, summed CPU;
- **proof-verify** is `proof_verify_inproc_ms`: the auditor's in-process proof verification;
- **aggregate** is `aggregate_inproc_ms`: the homomorphic sum. It is recorded in whole milliseconds (4, 10 and 29 per repetition), so its per-record values carry about ±0.0002 ms of rounding.

| Candidates | n | proof-gen ms/record | proof-verify ms/record | aggregate ms/record | committed_tps | p50 ms | p99 ms | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 10 | 1.6137 | 0.1757 | 0.00133 | 924.606 | 146.739 | 208.238 | 2026-09-30-mp-1k |
| 10 | 5 | 3.9787 | 0.3950 | 0.00333 | 536.489 | 230.943 | 488.809 | 2026-10-01-mp-1k-cand10 |
| 28 | 5 | 10.9910 | 1.0843 | 0.00967 | 297.175 | 455.482 | 664.171 | 2026-10-01-mp-1k-cand28 |

**Linear fit against the candidate count** (least squares on the three medians, y = a + b·c):

| Timing | intercept a | slope b (per candidate) | R² |
|---|---|---|---|
| proof-gen ms/record | 0.0614 | 0.39046 | **0.99999** |
| proof-verify ms/record | 0.0202 | 0.03796 | **0.99992** |
| aggregate ms/record | −0.0001 | 0.000348 | **0.99989** |
| committed_tps | 910.6 | −23.18 | 0.836 |
| 1 / committed_tps (ms of window per record) | 0.810 | 0.0924 | 0.990 |
| p50 ms | 98.9 | 12.77 | 0.999 |

**Reading:**
- The three cryptographic timings are **linear in the candidate count**: R² ≥ 0.9998 over a 7x range, with intercepts near zero. Proof generation costs about 0.39 ms and verification about 0.038 ms of CPU per candidate per record, as expected when every candidate slot carries one ElGamal ciphertext and one CDS disjunctive proof.
- **Throughput is not linear in the candidate count** (R² 0.84). It falls from 925 to 536 to 297 TPS, a 3.1x fall for 7x the candidates.
- The window time per record, 1/TPS, is close to linear (R² 0.99) with a large fixed part: about 0.81 ms per record regardless of candidates, plus 0.092 ms per candidate. That is the ordering and commit path plus a per-candidate cost of chaincode verification. The TPS ratio 924.6 : 297.2 is 3.1x, against 6.8x for proof generation.

**Caveats.** Three points only.

The 4-candidate row is from a different night (2026-09-30), on the same build and machine. It has n = 10 against n = 5, and 3 host-flagged repetitions out of 10.

Both of tonight's rows are host-contended: the user's browser was running, and 3 of 5 and 2 of 5 measured repetitions were flagged, with the rerun used. Their TPS medians may understate the uncontended values somewhat: cand10's two unflagged measured repetitions ran at 636 and 602 TPS. The per-record CPU timings are CPU time, not wall time, and are much less sensitive to that than throughput.

The ledger grows with the candidate count too. The peer volume after 7 elections of 21,000 records was about 13 KB per record at 4 candidates (security run), 20.9 KB at 10 and 46.9 KB at 28. The preflight's fixed 12,000 bytes per record holds only at 4 candidates.
