## 5. Contention

The wizard marks a repetition **contended** when either of its two host samples (taken before generate and after verify) shows host CPU above 25 % or guest `load1` above 0.25 x 16 = 4.0. The two halves are reported separately here: **host** = a Windows host CPU sample over 25 %; **guest** = a WSL guest `load1` sample over 4.0. From 10K up the guest half is mostly the run's own generate and verify work (16 auditor threads) still in the 1-minute load average, not outside load.

Measured repetitions flagged: **5 of 5** by the wizard's rule; **host CPU: 1**, **guest load: 5** (a repetition can carry both). Highest host CPU sample on a measured repetition: 25.5 %.

Every measured repetition is flagged on guest load (self-load, load1 7.22 to 10.1). **One host flag**: m5's end sample, 25.5 %, taken after the repetition's verify; its start sample is 3.6 %. Every other host sample on a measured repetition is under 18 %.

## 8. Caveats

The host samples are two points per repetition (before generate, after verify), not a trace across the submission window: a spike between them is invisible, and a flagged sample can fall outside the window it is charged to. Host CPU is the Windows host's total. The desktop was not idle: at 01:52 local the study log recorded Docker Desktop's UI (about 1.3 cores), Brave (about 1.2 cores across its processes) and an `mpv` video player running, none started by the study. `ledger_bytes_delta` is empty in every run (no producer for the column on this path). One build, one machine, one single-orderer test network. `docs/CLAIMS.md` and `docs/manuscript-amendments.md` were not edited. At n = 5 one stall moves the p99 mean (676.4 ms) far from the median (417.186 ms); quote the median.
